# 0.41.14 — BM place and responder review

The offline BM review recognizes Kenderesen and the karcagi, kisújszállási and hódmezővásárhelyi adjectives. Responding-brigade lists remain distinct from incident-place mentions, including the makói and hódmezővásárhelyi construction. Place adjectives in other contexts remain eligible for review.

## Upgrade

Update in HACS and restart Home Assistant. No card regeneration or configuration changes are required.

## Validation

All 166 local BM tests passed, including both observed RSS samples, responder-list separation and non-responder uses. The review does not verify incident coordinates or make automatic alert decisions.
